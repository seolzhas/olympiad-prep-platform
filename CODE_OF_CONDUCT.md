# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) as its Code of Conduct. The summary below explains how it applies here.

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be free from harassment for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity, level of experience, education, family income, nationality, language, religion or sexual orientation.

Our main users are children and teenagers. Their safety comes first.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind, patient and respectful, especially with beginners and students.
- Give and accept constructive feedback.
- Use clear, simple language; help people who write in Russian, Kazakh or English.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and learn from them.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Insults, harassment, discrimination or personal attacks.
- Any sexual content or advances, and any attempt to contact a minor privately.
- Publishing anyone's personal information without permission.
- Adding ads, tracking or paid barriers for students.
- Any other conduct that could harm a child or put a user at risk.

## Child protection

- Student accounts do not have public profiles or private messaging with other users.
- Contributors never ask students for personal data, photos or contact details.
- Any concern about a child's safety is handled immediately by the maintainers and, where needed, reported to the appropriate authorities.

## Reporting

Report problems by opening a confidential issue or contacting the maintainers listed in `README.md`. All reports are reviewed promptly and kept confidential.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, code or content, and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone who breaks this Code of Conduct. Consequences follow the Contributor Covenant enforcement guidelines: correction, warning, temporary ban, permanent ban.

## Scope

This Code applies in all project spaces — the repository, issues, pull requests, chats and events — and when someone represents the project in public.
