# Privacy and Data Policy

Our users are mostly children aged 13–18. We protect their data by collecting as little as possible and never using it for profit.

## What we collect

| Data | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Username or e-mail, password (stored only as a hash) | To sign in |
| Age range and grade (optional) | To suggest the right level |
| Learning progress (completed modules, answers) | To show the student their progress |

We do **not** collect full names, addresses, phone numbers, photos, location or ID numbers.

## What we never do

- We never sell or share personal data with third parties.
- We never show ads or use ad trackers.
- We never use student data to build marketing profiles.

## How we protect data

- All traffic uses HTTPS (encrypted connection).
- Passwords are stored only as secure hashes.
- Access to the database is limited to the maintainers.
- Regular backups; data is stored on servers we control.

## Consent and control

- Students under 18 sign up with the consent of a parent or guardian.
- Users can see, download or delete their account and data at any time.
- Data of deleted accounts is removed within 30 days.

## Law

We follow the Law of the Republic of Kazakhstan "On Personal Data and Its Protection" and widely accepted principles of data minimisation.

## Contact

Questions or requests about data: open an issue or contact the maintainers listed in `README.md`.
